    Clinical support only. AMEWRIN is a decision-support aid trained on a research dataset from Abia State PHC facilities. It does not replace clinical judgement, and any woman with severe symptoms (seizure, BP ≥160/110, visual disturbance, epigastric pain) should be referred immediately regardless of the model's output.
    AMEWRIN: Machine Learning-enabled Maternal Early Warning and Referral Intelligence Network for hypertensive pregnancy in Abia State primary health care facilities. Diagnosis model: multinomial logistic regression (4 classes), trained on 717 Abia State antenatal records plus 52,000 supplementary synthetic maternal-health records (Africa-region preeclampsia and WHO ANC guideline-structured data, used to test generalisation). Referral model: binary logistic regression, trained on the 717 Abia State records only, since testing showed the larger supplementary dataset degraded referral discrimination.
